## Real gap found and fixed (not in the original plan)

Success never cleared `Book.error_message` — a book that failed then
later succeeded (via retry, or any phase) would keep showing its last
error message alongside a `"done"` status. Fixed by clearing it at the
start of every phase's run. Caught by writing the retry-lifecycle test,
not by a pre-existing check.
