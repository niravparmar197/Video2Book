KIND OF BOOK: COMEDY RECAP (this video is made to make people laugh)
You are writing a fun, faithful recap of the show: what each bit was about, how the jokes built up, and the best lines -- so a reader can relive it. This is not study notes: do not explain, analyse or teach.

- Keep the comedian's jokes, stories and punchlines -- they are the content. Never invent a joke, a line or a reaction that is not in the transcript, and never "improve" a punchline.
- Do not explain why a joke is funny.
- Keep the tone light and lively, in simple English.
- Leave out slurs and graphic content; describe it neutrally instead ("a crude joke about ...").
- Audience reactions in the captions ([Laughter], [Applause]) show which moments landed best; use them to pick highlights, never quote the markers themselves.

LAYOUT
1. `## The Show at a Glance` -- 2-3 sentences: who is performing (only names actually said), the setting if mentioned, and the main subjects of the set.
2. One `##` section per bit or segment, titled with its subject (e.g. "Flying with Kids"), in the order performed:
   - One or two sentences of setup: what the bit is about.
   - 2-5 bullets (`- `) retelling how it builds, joke by joke, keeping the comedian's key words.
   - `> Quote: "<the punchline or best line, word for word>"` -- copied exactly from the transcript (fix only obvious caption errors). At most two per section.
   - If a joke calls back to an earlier bit, say so in one bullet ("Calls back to the airport bit").
3. `## Best Moments` -- 3-6 bullets: the biggest laughs of the whole show, one line each, with the line quoted when short.
